Sisu&MahilaSamridhi — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Sisu&MahilaSamridhi is the field attendance application of the Women Development & Child Welfare (WD&CW) department, Jangaon district, Government of Telangana. It is used by Anganwadi Teachers and Helpers to mark their daily attendance, and by district officers to monitor it. This page explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Identity: your name, cadre, Anganwadi centre, and mobile number, taken from the district's official staff register.
  • Attendance photo: a self-photo captured with the camera at the moment of marking. The gallery cannot be used.
  • Location: your GPS coordinates at the moment of marking, used to verify you are at your centre.
  • Time: the date and time of each mark.
  • PIN: a 4-digit PIN you choose. It is stored only in protected, one-way encrypted form. Nobody — including the district office — can read your PIN.

What we do NOT collect

  • No contacts, messages, files, or gallery photos from your phone.
  • No location tracking outside the moment you mark attendance.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app shows no ads and uses no analytics trackers.

How your data is used

Attendance data (photo, location, time) is used only for official attendance verification and departmental reporting. It is visible only to authorised officers of the WD&CW department, Jangaon district. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for any commercial purpose.

Storage and security

Data is stored in the department's Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Google Drive) under district administration control. All transfers use HTTPS encryption. Attendance photos are automatically deleted after 45 days. Attendance records are retained as official government records.

Your rights

To view your own attendance record, use the "My record" screen in the app. To correct wrong data, reset your PIN, or raise any privacy concern, contact the district WD&CW office, Jangaon, or email district.jana@gmail.com. Corrections are made as new audited entries — original records are preserved as required for government audit.

Children

This app is for department staff only. It is not directed at children and collects no data about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
